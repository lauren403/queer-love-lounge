Queer couples therapy in Sydney

Between the rent, the hours and the commute, who has time to cross the city for therapy?

Sydney's queer community is huge, and so are its waiting lists and its distances. A therapist who is perfect but an hour and two changes away is not really an option when you are all working full weeks.

Queer Love Lounge is online across Sydney and all of NSW, so you are choosing the right couples therapist, not just the nearest one, and never having to explain or justify your relationship first.

What you can expect

Built for queer relationships, not adapted to them.

No one hands queer couples a map. You have had to invent more of your relationship than most. So we start there, not with you explaining it. We help you name the miss: the moment one of you reaches and it does not quite land. Then we build the few moves that break the loop, so you are reaching for each other again. Not just fighting less; built for how you actually love.

The difference

Queer led & culturally safe

You will not have to explain what it is like to be you, or bring your therapist up to speed first. Led from inside the community, and a safe space for every culture, body and relationship.

The Lounge Method

Repair you can use while the hard moment is still happening, and room for desire to stay alive once it passes. Everything I trained in, reshaped for how queer relationships really work.

AMHSW · Completed Level 2 Training in Gottman Method Couples Therapy · Trained with Esther Perel

For the relationship

No blame, no taking sides. My client is not either of you. It is the relationship itself, the living thing between you all. I listen for what it has been trying to say, and help you find your way back to each other.

Online across Australia

Meet from your own couch: a capital city, a regional town, anywhere the map reaches. Secure video, no waiting room, no commute.

Why online works, especially in Sydney.

No crossing the bridge, no parking, no losing a whole evening to get to a 75 minute session. You meet from home, on a schedule that bends around shift work and long days.

From Newtown to the Northern Beaches to Parramatta and the Blue Mountains, the room is the same. And in a big city, working from home also gives you a quiet kind of privacy.

Good questions

Sydney couples ask us first…

Do you have an office in Sydney?
No. Sydney sessions are online, on purpose. In a city as spread out as Sydney, that means the Inner West, the Eastern Suburbs, the Hills or the Central Coast all get the same affirming couples work, without anyone losing an evening to traffic.
Does online couples therapy actually work?
Many couples find it does. Being in your own space can make the hard conversations a little easier, and structured couples work adapts well to video. If it is not working for you, we will talk about it.
Can this fit around demanding jobs?
Yes. Evening and flexible times, from home. All you need is a private room and a stable connection; we handle the rest.
How do we start, and what does it cost?
Start with a free 15 minute chat, or book straight into a first session (90 min, $240). Ongoing sessions are 75 min at $190, and nothing is paid when you book: your invoice and payment link arrive before the session. Sessions are online, by secure video. Private fee, GST free. Book below, or email lovelounge@queerlovelounge.com.au.
Do you see LGBTQIA+ couples who are married, open or poly?
Yes. Married, de facto, engaged, open, poly, and relationships of more than two. Marriage counselling for same sex couples is the same careful work as everything else here: the relationship is the client, and I do not take sides. You will not have to explain or justify your relationship first.
Can I see you on my own instead?
Yes, through Body Belonging Clinic, my individual practice. It is LGBTQIA+ affirming, online across Australia, and you can claim a Medicare rebate with a GP mental health care plan. If you are in a relationship and want to start on your own first, that is welcome too.
