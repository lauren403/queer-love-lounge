Queer couples therapy in Perth

We are a Western Australian practice, based right here in Boorloo.

Queer Love Lounge is led by Lauren Lynch, an Accredited Mental Health Social Worker based in Boorloo (Perth), so this is home turf, not a mainland service that treats WA as an afterthought.

Online across Perth and all of WA, from the coast to the regions. Perth's queer scene is close-knit, and online gives you discretion and real choice, beyond the handful of names everyone already knows.

What you can expect

Built for queer relationships, not adapted to them.

No one hands queer couples a map. You have had to invent more of your relationship than most. So we start there, not with you explaining it. We help you name the miss, the moment one of you reaches and does not quite land. Then we build the few moves that break the loop, so you are reaching for each other again. Not just fighting less; built for how you actually love.

The difference

Queer-led & culturally safe

You will not have to explain what it is like to be you, or bring your therapist up to speed first. Led from inside the community, and a safe space for every culture, body and relationship.

Gottman-trained, Perel-informed

The practical repair skills of the Gottman Method (Level 2), and Esther Perel's eye for desire and aliveness. Well-researched approaches, reshaped for how queer relationships really work.

For the relationship

No blame, no taking sides. My client is not either of you. It is the relationship itself, the living thing between you all. I listen for what it has been trying to say, and help you find your way back to each other.

Online, Australia-wide

Meet from your own couch. A capital city, a regional town, anywhere the map reaches. Secure video, no waiting room, no commute.

Online, from your own home

Why online works, especially in WA.

WA is vast, and affirming couples therapists are concentrated in a few Perth suburbs. Online means a couple in Bunbury, Geraldton or the Kimberley gets the same room as one in Fremantle.

And in a scene where everyone is a friend-of-a-friend, meeting from your own home is a quieter, safer way to do the work.

Good questions

Perth couples ask us first…

Are you actually based in Perth?
Yes. Queer Love Lounge is a Western Australian practice, led from Boorloo (Perth). Sessions are delivered by secure video so we can work with you across Perth and all of WA. This is a local practice, not a fly-in service.
Can you see couples in regional WA?
Yes. That is exactly who online is for. From the South West to the Pilbara and the Kimberley, if you have a private space and a connection, distance is not a barrier.
Does online couples therapy actually work?
Many couples find it does. Being in your own space can make the hard conversations a little easier, and structured couples work adapts well to video. If it is not working for you, we will talk about it.
How do we start, and what does it cost?
Start with a free 15-minute chat, or book straight into a first session (90 min, $240). Ongoing sessions are 75 min at $190, and once we have talked and found a time, a $50 deposit confirms it and comes off your fee. Sessions are online, by secure video. Private fee, GST-free.
