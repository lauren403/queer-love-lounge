Find the miss.

Every relationship has a rhythm, and most of yours is already working. The miss is the moment someone reaches and it doesn’t quite land. Eight quick questions show what’s working between you, where it can slip, and one thing to try saying.

Answer for yourself, then send it to your partner, or partners. Where your answers differ is often exactly where the miss lives.
