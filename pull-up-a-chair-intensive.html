Why a day

You warm up. Something true surfaces. Then the timer goes.

Then it’s a week of ordinary life before you pick it up again, half cooled. For some couples that slow rhythm is right. For others, it’s why nothing quite shifts.

An intensive is the opposite. Long enough to reach the miss, stay with it, and build the moves that break it while it’s still warm.

An hour a week

One unhurried day

Warm up, surface, stay.

No translating

A whole day that starts where you are. Not where a straight template assumes you are.

You won’t spend the first hours explaining your family, your history, or the shape of your relationship. We start with what’s actually happening between you, and use the time on that.

No one hands queer couples a map. An intensive is a stretch of time to draw a few of the ones you were never given. Your names, your pronouns and your words for your relationship, from the first minute.

What the day moves through

Before, the day, and after.

The Lounge Method in one stretch, with the two parts most couples never get: proper preparation before, and somewhere to land after.

Before

Your map

You each complete your intake and a short questionnaire in your own time, and you each have a private 15 minute call with me. Then I map what we’ll work on, so the day starts with the real thing.

1

Pull up a chair

A proper look at your relationship: its history, its strengths, and the loop you keep landing in. No performing.

2

Find the miss

We name the moment one of you reaches and the other doesn’t quite land, and slow it right down until you can both see it.

3

Reach again

We build the few concrete moves that interrupt the loop and turn you back toward each other. You leave with them written down.

After

Make it hold

Your written take home, then follow up sessions once ordinary life resumes, with the same short questionnaire again so you can both see what moved.

Breaks are built in, and we pause whenever the day needs it. Nobody here is the problem. The loop is.

Honest answers

The questions couples ask first.

Is an intensive actually better than weekly sessions?

It depends on the couple. An hour a week can end just as you reach the real thing. An intensive gives you an unhurried block to move through the whole arc at once. For some couples that suits better. For others, weekly sessions do. On your free 15 minute call we work out honestly which fits you.

Do we both need to be queer to come?

No. Queer Love Lounge is queer led, which means the room is built without assumptions about who you are or how you love. Two of you or more than two, monogamous, open or poly, any orientation or gender. You’re welcome exactly as you are.

Can we claim it on Medicare or private health?

Not on Medicare. Couples and relationship therapy isn’t rebatable under Medicare, which covers individual mental health treatment. Some private health extras pay a benefit for sessions with an Accredited Mental Health Social Worker, including couples sessions. Funds usually allow one claim per person per day, so an intensive is mostly self funded: you may be able to claim one session each, per day. Check with your fund before you book. The intensive is a flat private fee, GST free, with a payment plan if you need one.

What times does an intensive run?

A full day runs 9:00 to 3:30 Perth time, which is midday to 6:30pm in Sydney and Melbourne during daylight saving. That’s four blocks of work with two breaks and a proper lunch away from the screen. The Weekend is two days in a row with the same shape. The Reset runs 9:30 to 12:45.

Is it online or in person?

Online across Australia, with in person times in Perth being planned. Many couples find online just as deep. You’re in your own space, breaks happen when the day needs them, and it opens the work to couples anywhere in the country.

What if things are really raw right now?

That’s often when an intensive helps, but not always. If one of you feels frightened of the other, an intensive isn’t the safe first step, and I’ll help you find the right support instead, starting with 1800RESPECT. The free call is where we make sure the format fits where you actually are.

What happens after the intensive?

You leave with a written take home: your miss, your moves and your next steps. The One Day and the Weekend include follow up sessions once ordinary life resumes, so the moves hold. If you want to keep going after that, ongoing sessions are $190 for 75 minutes.

How do we start?

Join the waitlist for one of the dates, or book a free 15 minute call. On the call we talk about what’s happening, which format fits, and anything about cost or dates. If it’s a yes, a 50% deposit holds your date and the questionnaires come to you.
