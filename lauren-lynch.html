Who I am

From inside the community, and trained for the room.

I am a Yorta Yorta and Boonwurrung woman and an Accredited Mental Health Social Worker, with about fifteen years in the room. I work online with couples and relationships across Australia, from Boorloo (Perth), on Whadjuk Noongar Country.

I know what it is like to walk into a therapy room and spend the first while explaining your own life before anyone can help you with it. So I built one where you do not have to.

How I work

The relationship is my client.

No one hands queer couples a map. You have had to invent more of your relationship than most, so that is where we start.

The core

No sides

My client is not either of you. It is the relationship, the living thing between you all. I listen for what it has been trying to say.

Repair you can use

The practical repair skills of the Gottman Method. I have trained in Levels 1 and 2, and reshaped them for how queer relationships really work.

Room for desire

Informed by Esther Perel's work on desire and aliveness, so the work is about more than fighting less.

Every kind of us

Monogamous, open, poly and more than two. Trans and nonbinary partners. Married, newly together, or one of you is changing.
