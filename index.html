By & for us

No one hands queer couples a map. You've had to invent more of your relationship than most — who you are to each other, what family means, how you love out loud. Most therapy wasn't built for that. Here, it's the starting point, not the thing you have to explain — so we skip the explaining and get to the work that counts: the reaching, the missing, the finding your way back.

Finding the miss · understanding your pattern

You haven't fallen out of love. You've fallen out of sync.

The same argument on a loop. The silence that's easier than the fight. The distance that crept in while you were all just getting through the week.

It's rarely about not caring.

It's the miss — the moment one of you reaches and doesn't quite land.

It runs on a loop you can't see from the inside. My job is to help you name it together, build the few moves that break it, and get you reaching for each other again — not just fighting less.

Your first session

Your first hour, unhurried.

This is the heart of the Gottman work I'm trained in: the couples who last aren't the ones who never rupture — they're the ones who repair, and find their way back within the hour. That's a skill, and it's learnable.

01

All of you, together

We meet over secure video — you and your partner or partners, from your own space. Comfortable clothes, a cup of tea, and the names, pronouns and words you use for your relationship — all welcome.

02

We start with your story

No diving into the deep end. We begin with how you got here — your history together, what's changed, what's been happening lately — so we can name the strengths you're already standing on, and the pattern that keeps tripping you up.

03

The pattern, not the person

I'm not here to decide who's right. When things go wrong, it's rarely one person — it's the cycle you're caught in together. My job is to help you step outside it and see it clearly.

04

You leave with a map

You'll leave with the start of a map — a clearer sense of what's driving the distance between you, what's already working, and a first step you can take toward each other.

Good questions

Before you book — honest answers.

Do you work with open and polyamorous relationships?
Yes. Every relationship structure is genuinely welcome here — monogamous, open, poly, solo-poly, and relationships with more than two people — and you won't have to explain or justify yours before we can begin.
Do I have to be queer to see you?
Not at all. Queer Love Lounge is queer-led — built from lived experience, and without the usual assumptions about who you are or how you love. That lens makes it a safe, genuinely useful place for any couple. It's especially suited to relationships where someone is questioning or exploring their sexuality or gender, couples staying close through a change in who one of you is, and anyone who simply wants a therapist who won't assume. You're welcome exactly as you are.
One of us is exploring their sexuality or gender — can you help?
Yes — this is some of the most meaningful work I do. When one person is questioning their identity, a relationship can hold real uncertainty and deep love at the same time. I help you stay connected and honest through it, with room for whatever you each discover and no agenda about where it lands.
Does online therapy really work for couples?
For most couples, yes — and being at home often makes the harder conversations feel a little safer. Structured, evidence-based couples work translates well to video, and it's how Queer Love Lounge is built: online, wherever you are in Australia, from a space where you already feel comfortable.
What if only one of us is ready to start?
That's completely normal — often one person reaches out first. Everyone's welcome exactly as you are, and the free 15-minute chat is a low-stakes way to test the water together.
How do we start, and what does it cost?
Start with a free 15-minute intro call, or book straight into a first session (90 min, $240). Ongoing sessions are 75 min at $190.
