The miss

You have not fallen out of love. You have fallen out of sync.

It is rarely about the dishes. One of you reaches. It does not land. So one asks harder and the other goes quieter. That is the miss. Every couple has one. Almost nobody gets to see theirs from the outside.

In the room we slow it right down until you can both see it. Then we build the few moves that get you reaching for each other again. Not just fighting less. Reaching again.

Good questions

Before you book, honest answers.

Do you work with open and polyamorous relationships?

Yes. Monogamous, open, poly, solo poly, and relationships with more than two people are all genuinely welcome. You will not have to explain or justify yours before we begin.

Do you see married same sex couples?

Yes. Married, engaged, de facto or together for decades, you are welcome here. Marriage counselling for same sex couples is the same careful work as all my couples therapy. The relationship is the client, and I do not take sides. Some married couples come when things feel stuck. Others come because they want to keep it good.

One of us is exploring their sexuality or gender. Can you help?

Yes, and it is some of the most meaningful work I do. A relationship can hold real uncertainty and deep love at the same time. I help you stay connected and honest through it, with no agenda about where it lands.

Can we see you in person?

Yes. I see couples in person in Mosman Park on Mondays, and online by secure video across Australia on the other days. Choose whichever suits you when you book, or tell me on the free chat. I send the address with your booking.

Does online therapy really work for couples?

Many couples find it does. Being in your own space can make the hard conversations a little easier, and structured couples work adapts well to video. If it is not working for you, we will talk about it.

What if only one of us is ready?

That is common. One person usually reaches out first. The free chat is a low stakes way to test the water together, and nobody has to commit to anything on it.

Do I have to be queer to see you?

No. The Lounge is queer led, which means no assumptions about who you are or how you love. That makes it a useful room for any couple who wants a therapist who will not assume.

Do we have to do the free chat first?

No. If you have read this far and you are ready, book the first session directly. You will complete a short intake before we meet, and I read it first. The free chat is there for couples who want to check the fit before committing, and if you take it, we book your first session before we hang up.

What does the free chat actually cover?

What has been happening, what you are hoping for, and whether we are a fit. I will also ask a few questions to make sure couples work is the right and safe next step for each of you. Then we find a time, if you want one.

Is couples therapy right for everyone?

Nearly everyone, and I would rather be honest about the exceptions. Couples work is for the loops two people fall into together. It is not the right starting point if one of you feels frightened of the other, or if someone needs urgent care right now. In those situations I would suggest a conversation on your own first, with me or with 1800RESPECT, and I will help you find the right door. I also do not see people under 18, or write reports for court. For everything else, from the argument on repeat to the quiet distance nobody is named, you are welcome here.

Why do I need to create a login to book?

Bookings run through the secure client portal, part of Body Belonging Clinic, where your consent forms and notes are held privately. It takes about two minutes: name, email, mobile and a code by text. You only do it once.
