The miss

You haven't fallen out of love. You've fallen out of sync.

It's rarely about the dishes. One of you reaches. It doesn't land. So one asks harder and the other goes quieter. That's the miss. Every couple has one. Almost nobody gets to see theirs from the outside.

In the room we slow it right down until you can both see it. Then we build the few moves that get you reaching for each other again. Not just fighting less. Reaching again.

Good questions

Before you book, honest answers.

Do you work with open and polyamorous relationships?

Yes. Monogamous, open, poly, solo poly, and relationships with more than two people are all genuinely welcome. You won't have to explain or justify yours before we begin.

One of us is exploring their sexuality or gender. Can you help?

Yes, and it's some of the most meaningful work I do. A relationship can hold real uncertainty and deep love at the same time. I help you stay connected and honest through it, with no agenda about where it lands.

Does online therapy really work for couples?

Many couples find it does. Being in your own space can make the hard conversations a little easier, and structured couples work adapts well to video. If it isn't working for you, we'll talk about it. If you're in Perth, in person times are being planned; ask to join the list.

What if only one of us is ready?

That's normal. One person usually reaches out first. The free call is a low stakes way to test the water together, and nobody has to commit to anything on it.

Do I have to be queer to see you?

No. The Lounge is queer led, which means no assumptions about who you are or how you love. That makes it a useful room for any couple who wants a therapist who won't assume.

Do we have to do the free call first?

No. If you've read this far and you're ready, book the first session directly. You'll complete a short intake before we meet, and I read it first. The free call is there for couples who want to check the fit before committing, and if you take it, we book your first session before we hang up.

What does the free call actually cover?

What's been happening, what you're hoping for, and whether we're a fit. I'll also ask a few questions to make sure couples work is the right and safe next step for both of you. Then we find a time, if you want one.

Is couples therapy right for everyone?

Nearly everyone, and I'd rather be honest about the exceptions. Couples work is for the loops two people fall into together. It isn't the right starting point if one of you feels frightened of the other, or if someone needs urgent care right now. In those situations I'd suggest a conversation on your own first, with me or with 1800RESPECT, and I'll help you find the right door. I also don't see people under 18, or write reports for court. For everything else, from the argument on repeat to the quiet distance nobody's named, you're welcome here.

Why do I need to create a login to book?

Bookings run through our secure client portal, part of Body Belonging Clinic, where your consent forms and notes are held privately. It takes about two minutes: name, email, mobile and a code by text. You only do it once.
