Queer Love Lounge · six weeks

The Lounge at Home

Six weeks, one move a week, for the two of you, or the three or more of you. Read together, try one small thing, write on your own, then talk.

Before you start

This is for relationships where everyone feels safe to disagree. If someone in yours is afraid of another person in it, or is not free to make their own choices, this is not the place to start. If anyone is in danger right now, call 000. You can talk to someone today:

This programme is not therapy, and it does not replace it. It works well before sessions, between them, or while you decide whether you want them.

Where your words go

Nowhere. Everything you write stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing you write is sent to Lauren or anyone else. The page uses no analytics; only its fonts load from Google, and the weekly email below, if you choose it. Anyone who uses this browser on this device can open it, so if you share a device, clear it when you are done.

At the end

Bring this page

The answers each of you ticked “put this on our page” appear here, with everything you wrote together. Copy it into an email to yourselves, or bring it to a session.
