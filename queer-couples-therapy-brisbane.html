Queer couples therapy in Brisbane

In Queensland, the right affirming therapist might be three suburbs — or three hundred kilometres — away.

Outside the inner city, explicitly queer-affirming couples therapists thin out fast. Plenty of Queensland couples have never had a genuinely affirming option within driving distance at all.

Queer Love Lounge closes that gap. Online across Brisbane, the Gold and Sunshine Coasts, and regional QLD — the same warm, queer-led room whether you're in West End or well beyond it.

What you can expect

Built for queer relationships, not adapted to them.

No one hands queer couples a map — you've had to invent more of your relationship than most. So we start there, not with you explaining it. We help you name the miss — the moment one of you reaches and doesn't quite land — then build the few moves that break the loop, so you're reaching for each other again. Not just fighting less; built for how you actually love.

The difference

Queer-led & culturally safe

You won't have to explain what it's like to be you, or bring your therapist up to speed first. Led from inside the community, and a safe space for every culture, body and relationship.

Gottman & Perel-trained

The practical repair skills of the Gottman Method (Level 2), and Esther Perel's eye for desire and aliveness — well-researched approaches, reshaped for how queer relationships really work.

For the relationship

No blame, no taking sides. My client isn't either of you — it's the relationship itself, the living thing between you all. I listen for what it's been trying to say, and help you find your way back to each other.

Online, Australia-wide

Meet from your own couch — a capital city, a regional town, anywhere the map reaches. Secure video, no waiting room, no commute.

Why online works — especially in Queensland.

Distance is the whole story here. Online means a regional couple gets exactly the same care as one in Paddington, with no four-hour round trip attached.

It also means privacy. In a smaller town, not having to walk into a local clinic — or run into someone you know in the waiting room — matters.

Good questions

Brisbane couples ask us first…

Do you have an office in Brisbane?
No — QLL is online-only, and for Queensland that's a feature, not a limit. It's how a couple on the Sunshine Coast, in Toowoomba or in Cairns gets the same affirming couples therapy as one in the inner city.
We're regional — can you still see us?
Absolutely. As long as you have a private space and an internet connection, your postcode doesn't matter. Regional and remote Queensland couples are exactly who online is built for.
Does online couples therapy actually work?
For most couples, yes — and being at home often makes the harder conversations feel safer. Structured, evidence-based couples work translates well to video.
How do we start, and what does it cost?
Start with a free 15-minute intro call, or book straight into a first session (90 min, $240). Ongoing sessions are 75 min at $190, and once we've talked and found a time, a $50 deposit confirms it and comes off your fee. Everything is online, private fee and GST-free.
