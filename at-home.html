The six weeks

One move a week, in the order they happen.

Every relationship has a miss: the moment one of you reaches and it does not quite land. The six weeks follow that moment from the first second to the reach that repairs it.

Week 1

Pull up a chair

Sit on the same side of the table, and name your miss.

Week 2

Notice what arrived

Slow the first second down, before the reaction.

Week 3

Name what took over

Give the protective move a name, and get the wheel back.

Week 4

Pause without disappearing

Stop, say why, and say when you will come back.

Week 5

Find a way back to yourself

Know what brings you home to your own body.

Week 6

Reach again

Small, specific, and handing something over.

Each week holds:

  • A short reading to do together, ten minutes at most.
  • One Lounge Method card: the moment, the question, and what an answer could sound like.
  • One small thing to try that week, and a tick for each of you when you have.
  • Two unfinished sentences each of you completes alone, before you read each other's.
  • One question you answer together.

At the end you have one page, made only of the answers each of you chose to share. Keep it, or bring it to a session.

Your words

Where your answers go: nowhere.

Everything you write stays in the browser on the device you use. Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else. There is no ad tracking on the programme, and there never will be.

If you share a device, there is one button to clear it all. Queer people have had enough of their lives turned into data. This is a room, not a database.

Good questions

Before you start, honest answers.

Is this therapy?

No. It is a programme built on the same five moves I use in sessions, and it works on its own, before sessions, between them, or while you decide whether you want them. It does not replace therapy.

Can three or more of us do it?

Yes. Up to four people can use it together, and each person writes their own answers before anyone reads them.

Where do our answers go?

Nowhere. Everything you write stays in the browser on the device you use. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else, and there is no ad tracking on the programme. If you share a device, clear it when you are done.

Can we claim it?

No. Medicare and private health do not cover relationship programmes. The price includes GST, because a programme is not a session.

What if it is not right for us?

Write to me within 14 days of buying and I will refund you in full. No reasons needed.

Who is it not for?

Relationships where someone is afraid of another person in it, or is not free to make their own choices. If that is you, 1800RESPECT is on 1800 737 732, any time.
