Ten cards, and nothing has to be finished in one go.

Each one comes from the Lounge Method: noticing what arrived, naming what took over, pausing without disappearing, and reaching again. Some are for the hard moments. Some are for the good ones you’d like more of.

Pass is on every card, not buried in the rules. Keep the ones that land, put the rest back, and come again another week.

These cards are for ordinary relationship conflict. They are not a tool for coercive control, intimidation, threats, stalking, sexual pressure or violence. If someone is using them to press you, put them down.

These cards were built in my practice, for the people I actually sit with. Most of what gets written about love assumes two people, a closed door, and a script everybody was handed. None of that held up in the room. So these were written the other way round: for relationships with more than two people in them, for people who are out in some rooms and not others, for bodies that do not all work the same way, and for couples who do not live in the same house.
